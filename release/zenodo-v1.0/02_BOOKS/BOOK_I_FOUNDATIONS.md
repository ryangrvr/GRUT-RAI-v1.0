## 1 · What GRUT is, in one page

GRUT — historically expanded "Grand Responsive Universe Theory," a name the record itself
notes "overpromises more than the program does" (`GRUT_V1_PLAIN.md`) — is a
**constitutive-relational framework for physical theory**. Its proposal is a change of
primitive language, not a new fundamental Lagrangian: physical systems are to be described
through **constitutive response** — what a system does next depends on what has acted on it,
through a retarded kernel carrying its interaction history — and the gravitational vacuum
itself is treated as such a responsive medium rather than as an inert stage
(`GRUT_MODEL_FRAMEWORK.md` §1).

> **STATUS: ASSUMPTION (AXIOM)** — the responsive-medium stance is "a STANCE, explicitly
> not derived," priced +1 in the register precisely because founding status does not exempt
> an input from epistemic pricing (source: `provenance/claims.json`,
> `rung1_ontology_finite_memory`, tier `assumed`, ledger +1).

Three sentences frame everything that follows.

**First**, GRUT does not claim its primitives are derived from an assumption-free substrate.
It is carried, by owner decision, at the Newtonian standard: *given these declared
primitives, do the relationships among them produce correct physics, and eventually a
distinguishing prediction?* Primitives are not a defect; **undeclared or re-billed
primitives are** (`GRUT_PROGRAM_FREEZE.md` §2).

**Second**, the program's deepest commitment is not responsiveness. It is the **discipline
about responsiveness**: every claim tiered, sourced, priced, and falsifiable, with the
tiering enforced by code rather than prose, and with the program's own failures kept on the
record's face rather than edited away. The premise a reader should carry out of this book is
exactly that pairing — *a responsive vacuum with finite memory, where the ruthless
tier-marking matters more than the slogan* (`CHARTER.md` §§0–1, 6).

**Third**, GRUT's derivational ladder is **frozen** (owner decision, 2026-09-06). The
framework itself is live as a presentation and comparison object, but no new foundational
generation may be created "merely because the previous one failed"; the reopening keys are
specific, listed, and "hung where the program cannot reach them by itself"
(`GRUT_PROGRAM_FREEZE.md` §§1, 5).

A reader who wants the single most compressed honest summary of the physics is given, by
the freeze itself, one sentence with its grades welded on: *on a de Sitter background,
persistence came free; forgetting and direction were always paid for* — the first clause a
controlled derivation conditional on the background, the second an induction over the
examined record, whose universal form is exactly what a one-sided apparatus is least able to
check (`GRUT_PROGRAM_FREEZE.md` §7).

## 2 · The constitutive-relational worldview

Physics as ordinarily organized is **entity-first**: things exist, and externally specified
laws say how they move. GRUT proposes to organize the same physics **response-first**: the
primitive is the constitutive relation — a system defined against what it responds to, its
present behavior conditioned on its history through a retarded, causal kernel. On this
reading, dissipation, noise, decoherence, relaxation, and gravitational response are not
separate formalisms bolted together but **sector instances of one architecture**: one
influence-functional form, one fluctuation–dissipation lock, one passivity structure, one
kernel bookkeeping (`GRUT_MODEL_FRAMEWORK.md` §§1, 6).

Standard-physics background, distinguished as such: the mathematical carrier of this
worldview — the Schwinger–Keldysh (in-in) influence functional with a retarded kernel K_R
and a noise kernel N — is standard open-quantum-system machinery (Feynman–Vernon,
Caldeira–Leggett, nonequilibrium Keldysh field theory). GRUT's own record is emphatic that
adopting this carrier earns the framework nothing by itself:

**The influence-functional / Feynman–Vernon form.**
> **STATUS: RECOVERED (generic; u1: the form confers no GRUT-specific content)** — any
> local, causal open quantum system yields this form under coarse-graining; GRUT adopts a
> universal IR language, it does not derive one (sources: `provenance/claims.json`,
> `u1_form_universality`; `books/CORPUS_CHARTER.md` canonical table item 1).

So where does GRUT's content live? The record's own answer: **entirely in the declared
choices of the primitives table (§5 below) and in whatever consequences those choices
earn** (`GRUT_MODEL_FRAMEWORK.md` §3). That is the load-bearing honesty of the whole
program, and it is why the primitives table, not any equation, is the backbone of this book.

The worldview's central physical bet, stated at the strength the record supports:

**The gravitational vacuum is a responsive medium with finite memory.**
> **STATUS: ASSUMPTION, with REVERSED history on its face (the in-house "no memory time"
> computation was reversed; exact dS free-field theory forces infinite scale-free memory)**
> — the historically central commitment, retained as postulate only; its strongest form
> (single pole) carries no surviving discriminating consequence (sources:
> `books/CORPUS_CHARTER.md` canonical table item 18; `GRUT_MODEL_FRAMEWORK.md` §2, §7).

Note what that status block does: it keeps the founding bet **and** its reversal in the same
breath. That is deliberate, and it is the pattern for the entire corpus. The premise is not
"responsiveness explains everything." The premise is a specific, priced ontological wager
plus the commitment to mark, ruthlessly, what the wager has and has not bought
(`GRUT_ToE.md` Vol 0 — model history, ratified as current governance by `CHARTER.md` §1).

## 4 · What GRUT is NOT

**Not a Theory of Everything.** The ToE framing is retired, and has been since the clean
rebuild's first page: "GRUT is **not a Theory of Everything**, and the program has
**retired the 'derive everything' frame**. The filename `GRUT_ToE.md` is legacy (kept for
link stability)" (`GRUT_ToE.md`, front matter — **model history**; the retirement is current
record, restated by `CHARTER.md` §0: "This is not a theory of everything, and any draft that
starts sounding like one has drifted"). A prior book-length v4.0 ToE deposit exists on
Zenodo; the repo's own public documentation states plainly that it "is not the framework
described here, and it is substantially wrong" (`docs/WHERE_IT_STOPS.md` — model history).
What replaced the ToE frame, historically, was a closure/universality question — *given
responsiveness + locality + causality + open-system dynamics, what is forced and what is
imported?* (`GRUT_ToE.md` — model history) — and what that frame matured into, as of the
freeze, is the constitutive-relational framework of this corpus
(`GRUT_PROGRAM_FREEZE.md` §2).

**Not a predictive theory, as of this record.** The PREDICTED set is **EMPTY (nothing has
earned entry; Book IX governs entry)** (canonical item 21, status carried in §3). The word
"prediction" is reserved, corpus-wide, for gate-earned results only
(`books/CORPUS_CHARTER.md`).

**Not a derivation of standard physics.** Where GRUT reproduces GR, quantum mechanics, or
standard open-system results, the record marks the reproduction as recovery, with the
honesty note attached:

> **STATUS: RECOVERED (largely by identity; KERNEL-STANDARD, scoped)** — GR at zero-memory
> collapse, canonical table item 19; recovery-by-identity is compatibility, not
> correspondence evidence, because the executed machinery *is* standard machinery on the
> declared inputs (sources: `GRUT_PROGRAM_FREEZE.md` §3 RECOVERED;
> `GRUT_MODEL_FRAMEWORK.md` §4).

**Not self-certifying about its own method.** The discipline apparatus — register, ledger,
gates, adversarial screens — is the program's candidate contribution, and it is itself held
to the tier discipline:

> **STATUS: UNRESOLVED** — the method's novelty is graded in-register as a weakly novel
> synthesis of established methodology (every component prior art), with the "biggest
> contribution" over-claim retracted and independent external validation owed; a claim
> cannot be banked on self-verification (source: `provenance/claims.json`,
> `method_novelty`, tier `to-derive`).

**Not an ontology to defend.** The governing V2 charter is explicit: every branch is a
constrained classification problem with first-class failure states — "never an ontology to
defend" (`CHARTER.md` §8). Speculative furniture — the responsive-vacuum picture as
organizing intuition, self-recording/universal-refresh, black-hole saturation, "1Space" —
is permitted to exist and **barred from load-bearing**; 1Space in particular is UNDEFINED
on the record, all seven candidate definitions having failed non-circularity
(`GRUT_PROGRAM_FREEZE.md` §3, SPECULATIVE).

**Not a ladder that keeps climbing.** The stopping rule, owner's words, committed
governance: "No new RRT generation, model class, or foundational rung will be created merely
because the previous one failed" — a new generation requires an independently motivated
principle naming a specific phenomenon the existing framework cannot represent
(`GRUT_PROGRAM_FREEZE.md` §1).

## 6 · The status hierarchy — the eight-status ledger, and why it is the real commitment

**The vocabulary.** As of the freeze, every claim in the program is carried in an
eight-status vocabulary, replacing the earlier five-tier register vocabulary and its
"shame-tiering" connotations (`GRUT_PROGRAM_FREEZE.md` §2):

> `AXIOM` · `EMPIRICAL-INPUT` · `STRUCTURAL-SELECTION` · `DERIVED` · `RECOVERED` ·
> `PREDICTED` · `UNRESOLVED` · `SPECULATIVE`

The corpus's display labels (this book's bold status tags) map onto that ledger per
`books/CORPUS_CHARTER.md`: ASSUMPTION covers the three input kinds and always says which;
HYPOTHESIS displays SPECULATIVE; two record-outcome labels (CLOSED for computed refusals,
REVERSED for in-house results later overturned) and one absence label (UNMAPPED) complete
the set. Two rules give the vocabulary its teeth. **Scope qualifiers travel with statuses**
— "DERIVED (flat contract scope, ω ≫ H)" is a different claim from "DERIVED," and dropping
the parenthesis is how honest results become dishonest ones. And **PREDICTED is gate-earned
only**; it is currently empty, and no display label may drift a HYPOTHESIS toward it.

**The machinery.** The vocabulary would be decoration if it lived in prose. It does not. The
program's ground truth is a machine-checked register — `provenance/claims.json`, 74 nodes —
in which every claim carries a tier, sources that must resolve, a named
*overturning computation* (what would falsify it), and a signed `ledger_delta` pricing the
assumptions it adds or removes; the net is a blind sum printed by `provenance/validate.py`,
deliberately never hand-quoted in standing documents because hand-typed copies go stale
(`GLOSSARY.md`, translation layer). An anti-laundering gate blocks any net-positive claim
sold as a derivation (`provenance/auditor.py`); a live bank-time gate diffs every proposed
register edit against the last accepted baseline and surfaces substantive changes for
adversarial screening before a human accepts them (`provenance/bankgate.py`); and
disposition markers must live in the machine-scanned `sub_status` field, not in prose,
because a disposition the resident cannot see is a disposition that can silently soften
(`CHARTER.md` §7). Pre-registration is house rule: expectations are written and hashed
before an analysis runs, results live in a separate citing file, and the termination
condition itself was pre-registered outcomes-first and owner-signed (`CHARTER.md` §1.6).

**The calibration that matters.** The record states it about itself, and this corpus
repeats it wherever the machinery is described: this apparatus verifies **discipline, not
truth**. A wrong-but-well-provenanced claim passes; the blind ledger sum cannot catch a
physics error or a double count. The claim made for the register is that its contents carry
*provenance*, never asserted *correctness* (`GRUT_ToE.md` Vol 0 — model history;
`RESULTS_auditor.md`).

**Why this is the real commitment.** The record's answer is empirical, about itself. The
discipline is what caught the founding bet's collapse (P4's reversal was found by the
program's own instruments and kept on its face); it is what demoted the program's favorite
result rather than graduating it (`rung3` held at anchor-class against pressure in both
directions); it is what turned up the μ = 4/3 self-exclusion —

> **STATUS: CLOSED (self-exclusion: separate-universe consistency + low-ℓ ISW)** — the
> trace-only endpoint GRUT's own conformal coefficient naively suggests is excluded, a
> genuine self-exclusion the framework earned (source: `books/CORPUS_CHARTER.md` item 14)

— and it is what the one blind external-style adjudication on the record identified as the
program's actual product: "the transferable asset is the method, not the physics"
(`GRUT_PROGRAM_FREEZE.md` §7). The same discipline polices its own language: the register's
historical uses of the word "specialist" were audited, found to denote in-house AI-relayed
passes in the dangerous cases, and a standing rule now bars the unqualified word from any
public document (`GLOSSARY.md`). A program that does that to its own vocabulary is making a
specific claim about what it values; that claim — not the responsive vacuum — is what the
record most strongly supports.

The five-tier build vocabulary (shown / derived / derived-pending / assumed / to-derive)
remains the register's native grading and appears verbatim wherever this corpus quotes
register nodes; the charter mapping in `books/CORPUS_CHARTER.md` is the crosswalk, and no
node-by-node re-grading is performed by this corpus.
