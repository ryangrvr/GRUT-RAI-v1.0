## Status vocabulary (display labels, used inline at every substantive claim)

Every substantive claim carries a status block immediately after it:

> **STATUS: DERIVED** — one-line justification (source: `file`)

Display labels and their mapping to the register's eight-status ledger:

| display label | ledger status(es) | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **DERIVED** | DERIVED | exhibited and checked from declared inputs, at a declared scope |
| **RECOVERED** | RECOVERED | standard physics reproduced on the declared inputs — largely *by identity*; compatibility, not correspondence evidence |
| **ASSUMPTION** | AXIOM / EMPIRICAL-INPUT / STRUCTURAL-SELECTION | a declared input; qualify which kind, e.g. **ASSUMPTION (STRUCTURAL-SELECTION — chosen, not forced)** |
| **HYPOTHESIS** | SPECULATIVE | a proposed relation not derived and not validated |
| **UNRESOLVED** | UNRESOLVED | an open question the record poses but does not settle |
| **PREDICTED** | PREDICTED | gate-earned only; **currently empty — no claim carries this label** |
| **CLOSED (gate outcome)** | — | a computed refusal / no-effect / no-go on the record (e.g. the Γ_T closure); cite the instrument |
| **UNMAPPED** | — | the record contains no GRUT account of this topic at all |
| **REVERSED** | — | an in-house result later overturned by corrected analysis; part of model history, stated on its face |

Scope qualifiers are part of honesty: "DERIVED (flat contract scope, ω ≫ H)" is a different
claim from "DERIVED." Carry the scope.

**Supplementary vocabulary (added 2026-09-06 after the first-draft label census, defining
usages the books already carry in disciplined form):**

- **STATUS: EMPTY (nothing has earned entry; Book IX governs entry)** — the set-status
  rendering of canonical claim 21, used wherever a book states the PREDICTED set. It grades
  a *set*, not a claim; it is the only sanctioned use of EMPTY.
- **STATUS: SHOWN (register tier, …)** — the register's NATIVE tier for
  exhibited-and-checked-at-declared-scope, quoted where a book reports a register node in
  its own vocabulary. Maps to **DERIVED (scoped computed record)** in the display set.
  The full register-native crosswalk: `shown` → DERIVED (scoped); `derived-pending` →
  UNRESOLVED (anchor-class); `to-derive` → UNRESOLVED (posed, unattempted or unfinished);
  `assumed` → ASSUMPTION; `postulate` → ASSUMPTION (AXIOM).
- **Instrument-native grades, quoted** — audited instruments (the RAI Final Boss, the
  Dialectic Chamber) carry their own grading vocabulary. A book may quote such a grade as
  a status ONLY in the form **STATUS: <grade> (instrument grade, quoted at recorded
  strength)** — re-labeling an instrument verdict into the display set would misquote the
  record. These quoted grades never upgrade into DERIVED/RECOVERED. The same rule covers
  recorded audit dispositions quoted verbatim (e.g. "pending refutation if the signal
  consolidates", `SIGNATURE_AUDIT.md`) and fenced scoped vocabularies (the
  vacuum-cluster scope), each with its source named inside the block.
- **Qualifier rule** — a defined base label may carry a clarifying suffix or parenthetical
  ("UNMAPPED as a dynamical sector", "REVERSED-adjacent history, uncertified"); the base
  label governs, the qualifier narrows.
- **DERIVED dependency rule (global)** — every DERIVED claim must name its immediate
  premises and whether any premise is GRUT-specific, standard, or selected; where a
  first-draft block carries only its scope, the chapter-by-chapter audit adds the
  dependency line rather than the claim being read as unconditional.

This corpus is the **internal working edition**: it keeps the program's governance
vocabulary (laundering, fences, banking, W-0). A public-facing edition would translate
that vocabulary without weakening it; `books/READERS_MAP.md` is the bridge.

## Formatting conventions

- Every book opens with the working-draft banner: *"WORKING DRAFT — part of the GRUT
  working corpus; statuses per `books/CORPUS_CHARTER.md`; subject to chapter-by-chapter
  audit; nothing here banks."*
- Status blocks inline, immediately after the claim they grade — never deferred to a
  closing disclaimer.
- Each book ends with two sections: **Sources drawn from** (repo paths) and **Gaps in
  this book** (explicit, itemized).
- Citations point at repo artifacts by path (and external literature by arXiv id where
  the record already cites it). Prefer committed artifacts; if an uncommitted session
  record is load-bearing, mark it "(session record, uncommitted)".
- Readable scientific prose carries the narrative; the status blocks carry the honesty.
  Neither substitutes for the other.
