## The framework in six sentences

1. **Core model.** GRUT treats the gravitational vacuum as a responsive medium: physics is
   organized through retarded response kernels K_R and noise kernels N in a
   Schwinger–Keldysh influence action, constrained by causality (Kramers–Kronig),
   equilibrium detailed balance (KMS/FDT), passivity, and noise positivity.
2. **Inputs.** Every primitive is declared and priced: the medium stance itself, the
   system/bath split, the TT-only projector choice, the x = 0 point, the Born measure, the
   temporal orientation, the finite-memory bet (with its reversal on its face).
3. **Derived, at scope.** A one-loop TT kernel with H¹ = 0 identically (and a genuinely new
   even-degree cancellation identity behind it), an s = 5 spectral law, an exact de Sitter
   tail H²/4π, passivity/cone theorems, a linear-universe response no-go.
4. **Recovered, by identity.** GR at zero-memory collapse and standard open-system physics
   — compatibility on declared inputs, not correspondence evidence.
5. **Ruled out or refused, by the program's own instruments.** The μ = 4/3 endpoint
   (self-excluded), the observable tensor-friction route (computed REFUSE, the licensed
   value 62.7 orders below the observational slot), the ω ≲ 3.4H regime (unaskable at
   current declarations), and every audited resurrection of the undeclared-input pattern.
6. **What would count as a prediction.** A pre-registered quantitative consequence, not
   encoded in the inputs, surviving EFT/modified-gravity degeneracy, produced through a
   design gate (Book IX). The PREDICTED set is currently empty, and the corpus says so.
