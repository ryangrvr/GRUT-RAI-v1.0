# THE REALITY PROGRAM — phase charter

> **2026-09-07 · The post-publication phase of GRUT-RAI.** The published corpus stands
> untouched as *GRUT as presently understood* (frozen duplicate `FROZEN 9:6`; tags
> `zenodo-v1.0`, `v5.0-program-record`; Zenodo v5.0). This charter governs what happens
> next. It is a research-mode change, not an amendment to anything published.

## 1 · What this program is

Not a Theory of Everything, and not a resurrection — we do not yet know what GRUT
ultimately is. This is a **reality program**: a systematic attempt to discover what the
declared framework can become by confronting possibilities with mathematics, established
physics, and — where reachable — experiment, without deciding in advance what the answer
has to be. Reality has handed over a billion puzzle pieces; the job is to find which
pieces actually belong together, which are redundant, which are consequences, and which
are missing — not to declare the final picture first.

The mode change, stated as the owner stated it: the first phase asked *"what does GRUT
actually establish?"* This phase asks *"what dynamics can GRUT generate from its declared
structure?"* — moving outward (declared structure → dynamics → regimes → observable
consequences) instead of endlessly downward into foundations.

## 2 · The central principle (binding)

> **No question is excluded merely because it is difficult, unconventional, or currently
> unsupported. No hypothesis is accepted merely because it is attractive. No possibility
> is permanently turned on or off until its relevance can be evaluated by mathematics,
> comparison with established physics, or experiment.**

Corollaries: **OFF ≠ not-yet-investigated** and **ON ≠ true**. A possibility can simply
be **OPEN**. Elimination requires an evaluation on the record; activation requires a
surviving design gate; neither ever happens by fashion, difficulty, or hope.

## 3 · The ecosystem (four parts, kept distinct)

| part | role |
|---|---|
| **The Books** | tell people what the framework means (`books/`, published) |
| **The frozen record** | tells people what is actually established (register + frozen artifacts) |
| **RAI** | asks whether new claims survive hostile examination (`program/RAI_CAPABILITIES.md`) |
| **The dynamics program** | tries to generate things worth examining (this charter + the question ledger) |

GRUT asks what the picture could be; RAI asks whether the proposed pieces actually fit.
Neither needs to know the final picture beforehand.

## 4 · The pipeline (every investigation walks it)

    Question → candidate mechanism → formalization → calculation
             → comparison with established physics → experiment where possible
             → status update

Stage rules: a question enters the ledger as OPEN with a boxed gate question. Before any
expensive computation it receives a **design gate** (the demonstrated 11-step form:
inputs tabulated by status, a pre-registered decision tree, the discrimination question
answered from statuses before values). Calculations follow the SPEC form (outcomes
enumerated first; REFUSE first-class; verdicts computed at runtime, de-pinned).
Numerical/stochastic work is an *instrument* and gets instrument discipline: mutation
batteries, plants that must detect, convergence controls, negative controls — "the
ensemble found a fixed point" receives exactly the scrutiny a pass-label would. Every
outcome, including elimination, is a status update on the ledger, never a deletion.

## 5 · What still binds (inherited, not renegotiated)

- **The register discipline** — new claims are tiered, sourced, falsifiable, priced;
  the anti-laundering gate blocks assumptions billed as derivations.
- **The freeze, correctly scoped** — new *primitives* still require an independently
  motivated principle naming a phenomenon the framework cannot represent; new *dynamics
  from declared structure* is the sanctioned direction. The O2 interacting zero-mode is a
  named reopening key and may be walked through as such.
- **No history rewriting** — standing verdicts (the Γ_T closure, μ = 4/3, the frozen
  ladder) keep their recorded meanings; any reinterpretation must be a new, gated result.
- **W-0, preserve-the-failure, de-pinned instruments, the match-temptation rule,
  frontier-reserved fences** — all as recorded.
- **Vocabulary separation** — ledger dispositions (OPEN/GATED/ON/OFF/ANSWERED) grade
  *investigations*; register tiers and corpus statuses grade *claims*. They never mix.

## 6 · What success and failure look like

Success is not "GRUT becomes a ToE." Success is any of: a gate-surviving discriminator; a
derived structure not encoded in the inputs; a clean, computed elimination; a piece of
established physics shown to be a consequence rather than an input; or the discovery that
a question was wrongly posed. Failure is only the old failure: fooling ourselves. If the
eventual answer is that reality cannot be compressed the way the framework hoped, the
program must be capable of discovering — and publishing — that too.
